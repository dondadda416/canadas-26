      Data sources & updates

      Schedule. A check runs every night at 1:15 a.m. Eastern, after the last West Coast kickoff has finished. It records the previous day’s club results and what each Canadian did in them, rolls the fixture list forward a week and re-checks injury news. League games, starts, goals and assists roll forward game by game; the FotMob season ratings come from the r/CanadaSoccer “Canadian Men National Team … Summary” post and refresh when a new one is loaded.

      Club matchday. The matchday panel lists every club fixture involving one of these players today, with kickoff in Eastern time, and turns into the score once the game is played. This week opens recent results and the next few days. Player rows carry a matching tag.

      What changes. The club-form layer — club, league games and starts, FotMob rating, status badges, the weekly note, and the bubble list — plus the match list and each player’s note after his club plays — and, after Canada plays, the “since the World Cup” section. The World Cup group-stage numbers are fixed.

      Injury returns. The “back …” tag on an injured player is an estimate from club or press reports at the time of the sync, not a medical timeline; the card gives the source and date. “No date” means nothing has been reported.

      Spotting changes. Any player whose club line changed in the most recent sync gets a red dot by his name and a red edge on his row; the masthead counts them and the Updated chip shows only those players. The stamp inside a player’s club block gives the date his line last changed. Dots clear on the next sync that finds no change for him.

      Game log. Each squad player’s card lists every club match this season, newest first: opponent, competition, result from his side, minutes, goals and assists (saves and goals conceded for keepers), cards and the match rating. Greyed rows are games he was an unused sub for. A dash means that figure was not recorded, not zero; the season FotMob rating above the table is an average.
