      How this page updates

      Schedule. A check runs every day at 4:00 a.m. Eastern. It looks for the newest “Canadian Men National Team … Summary” post on r/CanadaSoccer (the author posts roughly weekly), so most mornings there is nothing new and the page is left alone.

      What changes. The club-form layer — club, league games and starts, FotMob rating, status badges, the weekly note, and the bubble list — and, after Canada plays, the “since the World Cup” section. The World Cup group-stage numbers are fixed.

      Injury returns. The “back …” tag on an injured player is an estimate from club or press reports at the time of the sync, not a medical timeline; the card gives the source and date. “No date” means nothing has been reported.

      Spotting changes. Any player whose club line changed in the most recent sync gets a red dot by his name and a red edge on his row; the masthead counts them and the Updated chip shows only those players. The stamp inside a player’s club block gives the date his line last changed. Dots clear on the next sync that finds no change for him.
